## Miss analysis (primary run)

`2026-11-07-synthetic-v1-covered-expenses-v1-lb6-oneoff5000-misses.json`

All 105 misses are understatements, from −15% to −28% (median −20%). All 105 have one cause: **bill_paid_as_transfer**. The local Enrich engine categorised committed bills paid by BPAY (insurance, rates and similar billers) as `transfer`, so they were excluded as own-account transfers. There are no overstatements and no other causes.

## Exploratory (not pre-registered; no bearing on the verdict)

Counting those BPAY bill payments as committed, and changing nothing else, gives 349 of 360 within 15% (96.9%). The method's shortfall on set v1 comes from categorisation, not from the averaging.

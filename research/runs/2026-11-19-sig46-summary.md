# SIG-46: gross-up from observed net pay, 19 November 2026

Pre-registration: `research/preregistrations/SIG-46.json` (`5046492`, before any code or run).
Gross-up 1.0.0 (`src/engines/affordability/grossup.ts`), tables `AU-RES-2025-26 v1` and
`AU-RES-2026-27 v1` (2026-27 Medicare thresholds provisional). Set v2.2 (`c520f371…`), 604 salaried
applicants with 3 or more months; as-of 2026-10-31 (FY 2026-27).

All accuracy figures are upper bounds (40424): X-3 makes net exactly gross less tax and levy,
salaried pay is identical to the cent, and end-to-end results inherit the T-pay limit.

  the ATO's Medicare example (Angie, $29,000, 2025-26) grosses back to $29,000, each within $1.

- **T4:** a mid-year 2026-27 version is pinned by date, and replay with the pinned version is
  byte-identical. The first run of this test failed for real (the trace said how the table was
  chosen); fixed in the code, not the test.
- **T5:** identical inputs give the same hash. **T6:** the 2025-26 table on the 2026-27 $135,000
  answer differs by more than $1.
- **Mutation:** with the 2026-27 levy rate set to 2.5%, 4 tests fail.

Target: 95% within 2%. **Not met** end to end; the control passed.

**Where the error is (diagnosis after the runs; nothing changed).** The gross-up adds essentially
none: observed net is within 2% of the true base-salary net for 55.1% of these applicants (333 of
604), against 54.8% for gross. The shortfall is observed income, not the tax inversion:

- 213 of the 252 misses overstate gross. Observed net includes pay that truth gross excludes
  (X-4: overtime and bonuses are net on top of base salary) and, for job changes, both
  employers' streams. By tag (share within 2%): new employer 16% (median error 73%), pay rise
  20%, coverage gap 26%, overtime 42%, salaried-irregular 44%, salaried-fixed 62%.
- 21 applicants have no observed recurring income (net 0), so their gross is unverified (T3).
- Longer histories do worse (3 months 61%, 6 months 54%, 12 months 50%): more room for pay
  changes inside the 6-month lookback.

## What this means

The gross-up is exact as apparatus against the tax tables, as X-3 predicts. End to end, gross is
only as good as the observed net income it starts from, and on v2.2 that is the SIG-16 stage.
Gross LTI and DTI from inferred gross carry the observed-income error with them; the badges still
follow net (D24).

## Limits

- X-3: no PAYG withholding, offsets or deductions. With LITO, real net is higher below about
  $66,667 and this method would overstate gross.
- 2026-27 Medicare thresholds are provisional; no salaried gross in v2.2 is below $55,000.
- Affordability is unchanged: it takes gross as an input (I-2). A caller must pass the gross-up's
  result; nothing in the live path does yet.

## What this result does and doesn't show

- **Within margin, and so the expected basis for "fit":** every undisclosed label (pooled, per
  type and at applicant level), unseen lender, reference noise, no card cue, account in grant,
  natural form and group, dishonour (applicant, fee and bounced debit), drift at applicant level
  in both modes, drift lines at the second bank, irregular pay and repaid-from-unshared.
- **Not shown clean:** identifying pay lines, card repayments and repayments in general by their
  amounts, and drift lines by recurrence. An experiment that must first identify those lines
  carries a stated caveat.
- **Fitness is decided per experiment by the set v2.2 independent review**, after the merge.
  Nothing uses set v2.2 before that review is posted.

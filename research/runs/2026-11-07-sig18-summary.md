## What this result does and doesn't show

It shows the method does what it was registered to do on set v1. It is **not** evidence of real
performance, and it should be read as a ceiling:

- **The grid is flat.** Every setting gives 1.00 and 1.00, so set v1 can't tell the settings
  apart: its repayments are exact in amount and timing.
- **The narratives are templated.** Every repayment names its lender plainly (for example
  `CASH CONVERTERS LOAN REPAYMENT`), and the generator uses the same provider names in the ground
  truth. Real narratives are abbreviated, truncated and inconsistent.
- **Cards repaid by BPAY are categorised as transfers (ENR-28).** Ten of the 20 undisclosed cards
  were found only because set v1's BPAY biller names say `CREDIT CARD`. Without that, those ten
  would be FN2 and recall would be 0.86.
- **What set v1 doesn't contain:** mortgages; undisclosed car or personal loans; repayments from
  an account the applicant didn't share (FN1 can't occur here); variable card repayments; and
  direct-debit or schedule data, which openfeed doesn't supply.

**For set v2 (SIG-54):** noisy and abbreviated lender narratives; BPAY billers without "credit
card"; liabilities repaid from unshared accounts; variable card and BNPL repayments; mortgages and
undisclosed loans; and lenders that are also merchants. The pre-registered miss causes (FN1 to
FN6, FP1 to FP3) are ready to tag those runs.
